# Hypothesis: Legal Document Analysis

## Context
Josh is a 20-year CA attorney; Haiku is good at extraction/classification.

## Potential Angles
1. **Due Diligence Documents** — Extract key clauses, risk flags, compliance notes
2. **Contract Summaries** — Generate readable abstracts of complex contracts
3. **Blockchain/Token Compliance** — Analyze docs for regulatory fit
4. **Proposal Review** — Flag inconsistencies in DAO/governance docs

## Constraint
- Haiku is *not* for legal advice or complex interpretation
- Use case: *processing* legal docs, *not* analyzing liability

## Viability
- Could target small law firms, legal ops teams, compliance teams
- Revenue path: \-300/month per customer for document batches
- Margin: High (mostly API costs)

## Questions
- Is there enough demand from small firms for automation?
- Can Haiku-level extraction work on complex legal docs?
